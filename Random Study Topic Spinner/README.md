# 🎡 Random Study Topic Spinner

A fun spin-the-wheel app where students add their study topics and spin to randomly pick what to study next — removing decision fatigue and making revision more engaging.

## Features
- ➕ Add/remove custom topics to the wheel
- 🎨 Animated CSS/Canvas spin wheel
- 🎯 Highlighted result display after spin
- 🔊 Sound effect on result
- 💾 localStorage to save topic list
- 🎨 Clean, responsive design

## Usage
Open `index.html` in your browser.  
Add study topics, spin the wheel, and let it decide what to study next.  
Topics persist across sessions with localStorage.
