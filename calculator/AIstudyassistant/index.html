Can you explain how the sodium-potassium pump works in the cell membrane?

Absolutely! The sodium-potassium pump is a vital mechanism for active transport in cells. Here is a simple breakdown:

  • It moves 3 sodium ions (Na+) OUT of the cell.
  • It moves 2 potassium ions (K+) INTO the cell.
  • Because it moves ions against their concentration gradient, it requires energy in the form of ATP.
