# 🎶 Focus Music Player (Lo-Fi Beats)

A minimal ambient study music player that plays looping audio tracks (lo-fi, rain, white noise) to help students focus. Includes volume control, track switching, and a live study timer overlay.

## Features
- 🎵 3–5 embedded ambient/lofi audio tracks (royalty-free)
- ▶️ Play/pause/next/prev controls
- 🔊 Volume slider
- ⏱️ Built-in study session timer overlay
- 🎨 Minimal, aesthetic UI

## Usage
Open `index.html` in your browser.  
Select a track, adjust volume, and start your study timer.  
Enjoy ambient sounds while focusing on your work.
